# The Quiet Compounding of Small Bets

> **Punchline:** Fund ten small bets a quarter, not one big one a year.

1. The Quiet Compounding of Small Bets

---

## One big bet a year starved every other idea

- Single annual moonshot absorbed 80% of the R&D budget
- Promising smaller ideas died waiting for next year's cycle
- One failure wiped out the whole year's learning

> Speaker note: set up the tension — concentration looked disciplined but was fragile.

---

## Ten small bets compounded to 3.4x the return

- $2.1M spread across 40 experiments
- 3.4x blended return vs 0.9x for the single-bet years

---

## Small bets fail cheap and teach fast

Failure at small scale is a tuition payment, not a catastrophe. Each dead experiment sharpened the next one, and the portfolio learned faster than any single project could.

---

## Fund ten small bets a quarter, not one big one a year

- Cap any single bet at 8% of the quarterly pool
- Kill on evidence, double down on signal
